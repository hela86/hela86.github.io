I work at the intersection of AI security, governance, and risk. My
focus is the practical layer most organizations struggle with: turning
AI frameworks — NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, OWASP LLM Top 10, and the
EU AI Act — into policies, assessments, and controls that teams can
actually use.

This site is where I publish that work in the open: risk assessment
frameworks, governance policy suites, threat-modeling kits, and the
tooling that supports them. Each project is built to be adapted, not
just admired — download it, run it, tell me what you'd change.

## Background

I hold the CISSP, CISM, and CRISC, along with the SSCP, and a range of
CompTIA credentials, and I'm currently pursuing the AIGP certification
alongside an MBA. Over the past year, I've focused specifically on AI
security and governance through ISC2's AI curriculum and hands-on
building.

## Beyond the portfolio
