## Problem
Most AI risk registers in the wild are either generic IT risk
templates with 'AI' bolted on, or technical evaluations that
don't translate to governance decisions. There is a gap in
the middle.
 
## Approach
- Mapped to OWASP LLM Top 10, NIST AI RMF, and the EU AI Act.
- Five-tab workbook covering inventory, risk, compliance,
  and executive summary.
- Methodology document including a deliberate 'limitations'
  section.
